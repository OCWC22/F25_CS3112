**Quicksort:**
- PARTITION: O(n) - Lomuto vs Hoare code
- Average case: O(n log n) - with randomization
- Worst case: O(n²) - sorted input, fix with random pivot
- QuickSelect: O(n) - for kth largest

**LeetCode Patterns:**
- "Kth largest" → Heap of size k or QuickSelect
- "Top K" → Same as above
- "Merge K sorted" → Min-heap of size K
- "Find median" → Two heaps (max + min)
