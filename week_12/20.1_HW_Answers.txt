Section 20.1 Homework Answers

20.1-1 - Out-degrees and in-degrees from adjacency lists

• Out-degree of every vertex: length of each list; summing lengths over all vertices is |E|. Time Θ(V + E).

• In-degrees: scan all lists and count incoming endpoints; also Θ(V + E).

20.1-2 - Adjacency-list & matrix for a complete binary tree on 7 vertices (heap indexing 1..7)

Edges (undirected) using heap relations i↔2i, i↔2i+1 where in range:

• 1: {2,3}
• 2: {1,4,5}
• 3: {1,6,7}
• 4: {2}
• 5: {2}
• 6: {3}
• 7: {3}

Adjacency matrix (symmetric 7×7): put 1s at those pairs (and 0s elsewhere).
